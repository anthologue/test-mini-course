Frontline Roles for AI Co-Worker Agents

This course is about frontline service agents: AI co-workers that talk with customers and visitors, the way a receptionist, shop assistant or guide would. You do not need any background in AI. Terms are explained in plain words as they come up.

What you will learn

  • What frontline AI agents are and what they can do
  • The roles they can play, from reception to retail
  • How human jobs can change, and how companies can support their staff

Will AI Take Over My Job?

When AI arrives at work, staff have real worries. Is my job still relevant? Will my pay be reduced? Is there still a way to move up in my career? Should I start looking for work somewhere else?

Companies can answer each of these worries with a clear step:

  • Upskilling: the company trains its staff in AI skills.
  • Job redesign: staff learn to manage and make use of AI, and are paid better for it.
  • A transformed career path: a new track for workers who are ready to work with AI.
  • Staff retention: keeping people through AI-driven pay, benefits and career opportunities.

In plain words

Upskilling
Teaching current staff new skills so they can keep doing useful work as technology changes.
Staff retention
Keeping good employees from leaving.
Quick check

Frontline Multi-modal Conversational AI Agents

The agents in this course are meant to be fast-response, lean-cost and multi-role: they answer quickly, cost little to run, and can fill several different jobs. Here is what makes that possible:

  • Two-way communication. They can talk, type and respond to touch, with a choice of voice.
  • A human look. A human avatar engine quickly creates varied, lifelike expressions on screen.
  • Conversation with reasoning. A suite of language models (the Aitom LLM Suite) understands what people say and replies, for example by recommending a product or giving a price.
  • Role skills. They come pre-trained in the skills of many human roles.
  • Insights. They can study their conversations with visitors and customers to show a business what people want.
  • Train once, use everywhere. One “brain” is trained once and then controls agents at every site and location.

In plain words

Multi-modal
Able to communicate in more than one way. Here: voice, text and touch.
Conversational AI
AI you can simply talk to, instead of clicking through menus.
LLM (large language model)
The kind of AI that understands and writes human language.
Avatar
An on-screen character that looks like a person.
Deployment
Putting an agent into use at a real place, such as a shop or an office.
Quick check

What does “train once, use everywhere” mean?

A single “brain”, once trained, controls deployments across all sites and locations.

Roles for Frontline AI Co-workers

This is a platform of AI agents trained for many kinds of engagement, from the back office to the front desk to the front line. The picture below shows nine roles. The list under it has the same information as text.

  • AI Restaurant Assistant recommends dishes to suit a customer’s taste. It might say, “Welcome! Let me recommend our most popular dishes to you.”
  • AI Knowledge Agent gives staff quick answers about the organisation, from company policies to how things are done. “Ask me about our corporate policies.”
  • AI Trainer acts as a virtual trainer in staff courses on work skills.
  • AI Virtual Receptionist receives and registers guests and tells staff when a guest arrives. “Welcome to our office! How may I help you?”
  • AI Concierge guides visitors to offices, restaurants and shops. “Please take the lift at Lobby B to go to the bank at Level 3.”
  • AI e-Services Agent helps people find and use online services, and answers questions about them.
  • AI Feedback Agent invites customers to share feedback. “Are you satisfied with our service?”
  • AI Product Agent helps customers with product questions and suggests suitable items. “What kind of scent are you looking for?”
  • AI Service Robot is a roving robot that talks to customers and leads them to product sections. “Let me take you to the Chocolates section.”

Together, these agents can fill gaps where there are not enough workers, and add to what the human team can do, so that customers get friendly, human-like service 24 hours a day.

In plain words

Concierge
Someone whose job is to help visitors find what they need and where to go.
Augment the workforce
Add to what people can do, rather than replace them.
Back office / front desk / front line
Work behind the scenes, work at a reception desk, and work face to face with customers.
Quick check

Which role gives staff quick answers about company policies?

The AI Knowledge Agent answers staff questions on enterprise knowledge, from corporate policies to operational procedures.

Human Job Re-design & Re-skilling

How can a person’s role change in a workplace where people and AI work side by side? The course asks three questions:

  • Can a role change from doing the work yourself to supervising AI co-workers?
  • Can staff be trained to check and set up AI agents so they perform as well as possible?
  • Can staff earn more when the AI agents they look after do well together?

If the answer to each is yes, AI becomes something staff work with and benefit from, which also speaks to the worries in the second section.

In plain words

Hybrid workforce
A team made up of both people and AI agents.
Individual contributor
Someone who does the work themselves rather than managing others.
KPI (key performance indicator)
A number used to measure how well work is going, such as customers helped per day.
Re-skilling
Learning different skills for a changed role.
Quick check

In a redesigned role, what might a staff member do?

A role can change from doing the work yourself to supervising AI co-workers and checking how well they perform.

AI Agents for Customer Service

Less workload for staff

Customer service officers currently ask users for feedback more than 100 times a day. An AI agent can take over that task, which frees the officers to give more focused service.

More hygienic feedback

Instead of touching a shared panel and risking the spread of germs, users can give their feedback by speaking to an AI agent, with no contact at all.

In plain words

Customer service officer (CSO)
A staff member whose job is to help customers.
Contactless
Done without touching anything.
Quick check

Why can giving feedback by voice be more hygienic?

Users speak to an AI agent instead of touching a panel, so there is no contact at all.

AI Agents for Office Concierge / Receptionist

An AI agent can welcome people at the door, taking over the hospitality tasks of a receptionist and working with staff when visitors arrive. It can:

  • Greet guests as a digital human, and find out the guest’s name, company and who they are meeting.
  • Direct the guest to the right meeting room.
  • Tell staff by SMS, WhatsApp or Telegram that their guest has arrived.
  • Help run the office, for example by adjusting the air-conditioning or preparing a meeting room.

In plain words

Digital human
A lifelike on-screen person that can talk with you.
Hospitality
Making guests and visitors feel welcome and looked after.
Quick check

Besides greeting guests, what can an AI receptionist do?

It can notify staff by SMS, WhatsApp or Telegram when a guest arrives.

AI Agents for Hospitality – Tourism & Attractions

At tourist spots, an AI visitor concierge can:

  • Serve visitors and tourists in different languages.
  • Introduce the features and highlights of an attraction or event venue.
  • Suggest extra purchases, such as drinks or souvenirs (this is called upselling and cross-selling).

In plain words

MICE venue
A place for meetings, incentives, conferences and exhibitions.
Upsell / cross-sell
Offering a better version of what someone is buying, or something extra that goes with it.
Quick check

What can an AI visitor concierge do?

It serves visitors and tourists in different languages and introduces the highlights of the attraction.

Challenges in Retail & Service Industry

People now shop in three ways: in a physical shop, online, and with a personalised AI advisor. This brings four challenges for shops and service businesses:

  • Shifting demographics. Who prefers a human, online, digital or AI touch?
  • In-store sales coverage. Do customers wait too long to be served, or to check a price or whether something is in stock?
  • Consistent service around the clock. Are there enough staff for long opening hours, and are customers happy?
  • Knowing what customers want. Is there enough data to understand them and make decisions?

In plain words

Demographics
Facts about a group of people, such as their age.
Retail
Selling goods directly to customers, in shops or online.
Quick check

Which is a challenge for shops and service businesses?

In-store sales coverage is one of the four challenges: customers may wait too long to be served.

Retail AI Agents

The idea here is engage everywhere: customer engagement with no friction. It has three steps:

  1. Put AI experts everywhere, for example one for apparel, handbags, chocolates and perfumes, plus one online.
  2. Help staff become trusted advisers, with AI agents assisting them in running the store.
  3. Let customers do ‘anything’ anywhere.

That last step means customers can:

  • Ask questions
  • Get recommendations
  • Check price
  • Check stock
  • Get personalised promotions
  • Find a product on a map
  • Go straight to checkout

In plain words

Frictionless
Smooth and easy, with nothing slowing the customer down.
Trusted adviser
Someone customers rely on for honest, helpful advice.
Personalised promotion
An offer chosen to suit one particular customer.
Quick check

What does “engage everywhere” mean?

AI experts are put everywhere, and customers can do ‘anything’ anywhere, from asking questions to checking out.
